# 14 - Customer / Renter User Guide

## Step-by-Step Renter Walkthrough

### 1. Registration & Verification
- Click **Sign Up** -> choose **Rent a Car**.
- Fill in name, email, phone, and password.
- Verify your email using the link sent to your inbox.
- Navigate to **Profile** -> **KYC Verification** to upload your Driving License photo.

### 2. Vehicle Search & Filter
- Browse available vehicles by city, brand, body type (SUV, Sedan, Luxury, Electric), or transmission.
- Switch to **Map View** to see car pins distributed across the city.
- Click **Near Me (GPS)** to discover cars closest to your physical location.

### 3. Creating a Booking
- Select your desired **Pickup Date** and **Return Date**.
- Review price breakdown (Daily rate x Total days + 15% platform service fee).
- Click **Reserve Vehicle (15-min Hold)**.

### 4. Making Payment
- Scan the generated dynamic UPI QR code using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.
- (On Mobile) Click **Open in Installed UPI App** to auto-fill the transaction.
- Enter the 12-digit UTR / Transaction Reference ID and click **Submit UTR**.

### 5. Check-In, Trip & Return
- Once verified by admin, view your booking under **My Bookings**.
- Upon vehicle pickup, complete the **Pre-Trip Inspection** (upload photos of odometer and vehicle exterior).
- Click **Start Trip**.
- At trip conclusion, complete the **Return Inspection** and submit your 5-star review for the host and vehicle.
