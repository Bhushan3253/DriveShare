# 01 - Introduction

## Welcome to CarSharePro (DriveShare)

**CarSharePro** is a commercial, production-ready full-stack software solution for peer-to-peer car sharing and vehicle rental operations. It enables car owners (hosts) to monetize their personal or fleet vehicles, while giving customers (renters) seamless access to vehicles on demand.

---

## 🎯 Target Business Models

1. **Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Marketplace**: Connect private vehicle owners with local drivers (Airbnb / Turo model).
2. **Dedicated Car Rental Agency**: Manage and scale a proprietary fleet of rental cars across multiple hubs or cities.
3. **Corporate Fleet Sharing**: Enable intra-organization vehicle reservations and check-in/return accountability.

---

## 🌟 Core Modules

- **Renter App**: Interactive geospatial map, real-time availability calendar, 15-minute reservation locks, UPI QR code checkout, digital pre-trip inspection, trip tracking, and verified reviews.
- **Host / Owner Portal**: Vehicle listing submission with compliance documents, pricing controls, custom availability calendar, active booking tracking, and earnings/payout ledger.
- **Admin Super-Dashboard**: KYC identity approval, vehicle inspection & moderation, 1-click UTR payment verification, payout tracking, and system metrics.
- **Cross-Platform Android App**: Native mobile app built with Capacitor supporting GPS location services, camera uploads, and deep-link UPI intents.
